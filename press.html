Boilerplate

Bottle Rocket Racing is a motorsports business development and sponsorship firm based in Chicago, Illinois. Founded in 2024 as the motorsports arm of Bottle Rocket Growth, BRR partners with elite race teams, including NASCAR teams, to build the commercial infrastructure that converts fan enthusiasm into lasting sponsorship revenue. The firm provides two service lines: Strategy and Infrastructure (CRM setup, outreach cadence design, prospect identification, playbook development) and Business Development and Pipeline Generation (active outreach, pipeline tracking, sponsorship proposal development, deal support).

About the founder

Mike Cubberly is the founder of Bottle Rocket Racing and Bottle Rocket Growth. Mike has spent over a decade building go-to-market systems for B2B technology companies, with prior roles at Verkada, Glencoco, Primer, and RevGenius. He launched Bottle Rocket Growth in 2024 and the motorsports-focused Bottle Rocket Racing the same year. Mike is based in Chicago, Illinois.

"Race teams deserve the same commercial sophistication as the machines they field. Bottle Rocket Racing exists to bring that level of rigor to the business side of motorsports."
