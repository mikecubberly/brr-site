About

The motorsports arm of Bottle Rocket Growth.

By Mike Cubberly, Founder • Published June 2024 • Last updated April 29, 2026 • Chicago, Illinois

Bottle Rocket Racing is a motorsports business development and sponsorship firm. The firm was founded in 2024 as the motorsports arm of Bottle Rocket Growth, a Chicago-based LinkedIn outbound agency and go-to-market consultancy for B2B startups. BRR partners with elite race teams, including NASCAR teams, to build the commercial infrastructure that converts fan enthusiasm into lasting sponsorship revenue.

The firm exists because race teams deserve the same commercial sophistication as the machines they field. The best teams in motorsports win not just with engineering, but with the organizations they build around their cars. Bottle Rocket Racing brings that same rigor to the commercial operation.

Founder

Mike Cubberly is the founder of Bottle Rocket Racing and Bottle Rocket Growth. Based in Chicago, Mike has spent over a decade building go-to-market systems for B2B technology companies. Prior to launching Bottle Rocket Growth in 2024, Mike held roles at Verkada, Glencoco, Primer, and RevGenius, where he continues to serve as Community Admin.

What we do

Bottle Rocket Racing focuses exclusively on motorsports revenue. The firm builds and runs the business development and pipeline generation engines that bring serious, sustained revenue to race teams. No fragmented retainers. No generic consulting. Just commercial execution at the pace the racing calendar demands.

Two service lines

Strategy and Infrastructure. The full commercial infrastructure a race team needs to operate like a serious revenue-generating business.

  • CRM setup and management
  • Outreach cadence design
  • Prospect identification and targeting
  • Playbook development and team training

Business Development and Pipeline Generation. Active execution. BRR identifies, contacts, and manages relationships with qualified brand partners on behalf of race teams.

  • Active prospect outreach
  • Pipeline tracking and reporting
  • Sponsorship proposal development
  • Deal support and close strategy

Three pillars of approach

Relationship First. In racing, everything is earned through trust. BRR approaches every potential sponsorship as a long-term relationship, not a transactional close.

Data-Driven Pipeline. Every touchpoint is tracked, measured, and optimized. Full visibility into commercial performance at all times.

Built for Speed. The racing calendar waits for no one. BRR processes are designed to identify, engage, and close at the pace the sport demands.
